This brief, company and product are invented test material. They are not a real business, customer endorsement or product claim.

## Brief

Create a private single page HTML demo for Mosslight Paper, a fictional small stationery studio. Audience: people choosing a thoughtful desk gift. Product: an undated paper notebook with a botanical cover. Goal: help the visitor understand the design and inspect a sample collection.

Deliver one responsive HTML page plus all local assets and editable source. Use warm ivory, forest green and ink black. Use a clear heading, product illustration, collection preview, short studio story and one primary action, "Explore the collection", linking to the collection section. Make the mobile arrangement deliberate, with readable type and keyboard accessible controls.

Generate original botanical illustrations. Label them as concept illustrations, not real product photos. Do not copy an existing brand. Treat sample names and copy as fictional. No price, stock level, testimonial, certification, shipping promise or real shop URL has been supplied; omit them rather than inventing facts. No forms, checkout, analytics, external fonts or network dependent assets. No deployment or external messages.

Settled: fictional brand, palette, audience, single page, local assets, private demo and primary anchor action. The builder may choose layout, typography from system fonts and illustration details within those constraints. Ask only if a consequential new gap blocks the agreed work.
